# Design learnings, pre-gate extract

The judgment of WeMod Web V0 section 29 §5 (V0 version 1793734041-0106, records L-01 to L-05), with everything that
shows a previous design removed: source canvases, scores, the design owner's quoted feedback, and the forms and
layouts the judgments were drawn from. Read this at the skill's Step 2.5 instead of V0 29 §5. The full section 29,
including §5 as written, opens after the exploration gate (your page narratives and section strategy exist).

Each learning is judgment with limits, never a rule. Write one line per learning that matches this task: what it
changes in your design, or why it doesn't apply.

## L-01 · One page thesis, proved differently by each section

- **Judgment:** a page concept is a claim to prove, not a headline. Each section proves a different part of that claim
  with a different kind of evidence. Different evidence gives the page rhythm; one claim gives it coherence. A section
  that shows no part of the claim can be left out without loss.
- **Useful when:** full-page or multi-section work whose job is to convince a visitor of one thing; deciding which
  sections exist and what each must show.
- **Not useful when:** element or section work inside the current page; task pages (download, legal, account) whose
  job is completion, not persuasion.
- **Evidence:** E1 (design-owner review). No user or test data.

## L-02 · A concept that only changes the first screen is a hero variant

- **Judgment:** when the hero carries the concept and the body falls back to finished, generic sections, the page is
  the same page with a different head. The test, for the sections meant to carry the concept: would this section work
  unchanged under another direction's hero? Derive those sections from the concept's own questions instead.
- **Useful when:** comparing directions; reviewing a full-page exploration; deciding whether a production section
  belongs in a concept.
- **Not useful when:** shared elements doing their job (navigation, the fast download path, trust, FAQ, footer,
  required SEO content); element or section work; a direction that deliberately tests one hero against the same body.
- **Evidence:** E1.

## L-03 · In WeMod, impact comes from composition, not from scale or decoration

- **Judgment:** impact that holds up comes from composition at WeMod's own type scale. Impact made from oversized
  headings or added decoration reads as off-brand or broken. Automated brand checks don't catch this; only looking at
  the render does.
- **Useful when:** any task that asks for a bolder, fresher or more creative look; translating a reference.
- **Not useful when:** it does not oblige a new composition. A clean, restrained page can be the strongest answer, and
  "bolder" is never a reason to add elements.
- **Evidence:** E1.

## L-04 · A hypothesis about a product mechanic doesn't carry a page

- **Judgment:** a page concept has to answer a visitor's doubt or desire (is my game here, is it hard, what do I get,
  can I trust it). A mechanic such as a hotkey is proof inside such a concept, not the concept itself.
- **Useful when:** choosing which explanation to pursue.
- **Not useful when:** the section that explains the mechanic, such as a how-it-works step; or a test whose evidence
  shows the mechanic itself is the barrier.
- **Evidence:** E1.

## L-05 · From a reference, take the moves, not the surface

- **Judgment:** list a reference's structural moves, drop its surface (texture, display type, decoration), rebuild each
  move from V0 parts at V0 scale, and keep only the moves that serve this page's concept.
- **Useful when:** a reference image or site is part of the brief.
- **Not useful when:** copying a reference's whole composition, whose structure answered another product's story.
- **Evidence:** E1.
